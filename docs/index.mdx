skcal tracks weigh-ins, body measurements, meals, and training, and gives you a
daily snapshot of where you stand against your targets. An agent logs things for
you and answers from your real numbers. Underneath, everything is a typed HTTP
API, a CLI, and a remote MCP server, so your own tools and agents can do the
same.

## What you can do

- **Log meals** from a photo, a pasted screenshot, or a plain-text description. skcal estimates calories and protein per item.
- **Log your body**: weigh-ins (with optional body-fat % and notes) and body measurements, or let a smart scale push them.
- **Track** calories and protein against daily targets, and weight against a goal, with cut, bulk, or maintenance phases.
- **Train** with self-adjusting hypertrophy mesocycles: sets, reps, and load progress from what you lifted and how you recovered.
- **Ask the agent** what to eat or how training is going. It reads and edits your log, so its answers come from your real numbers.
